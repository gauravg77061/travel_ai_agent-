Manali is a popular hill station in Himachal Pradesh known for snow, adventure sports, and scenic beauty. Tourists visit Solang Valley for skiing and paragliding.

Goa is famous for beaches, nightlife, seafood, and water sports. Popular beaches include Baga, Calangute, and Anjuna.

Jaipur, also called the Pink City, is known for historical forts like Amer Fort, Hawa Mahal, and rich Rajasthani culture.

Kerala is known for backwaters, houseboats, ayurveda treatments, and greenery. Alleppey is a famous destination.

Delhi is the capital of India and has historical monuments like Red Fort, Qutub Minar, and India Gate. It is also famous for street food.

Mumbai is known as the financial capital of India. It has attractions like Marine Drive, Gateway of India, and Bollywood.

Leh-Ladakh is known for high-altitude landscapes, monasteries, and bike trips. Pangong Lake is a major attraction.

Rishikesh is famous for river rafting, yoga, and spiritual retreats. It is also known as the Yoga Capital of the World.

Agra is famous for the Taj Mahal, one of the Seven Wonders of the World. It also has Agra Fort and Fatehpur Sikri.

Varanasi is one of the oldest cities in the world, known for the Ganga ghats and spiritual rituals.

Shimla is a hill station known for colonial architecture, Mall Road, and pleasant weather.

Udaipur is known as the City of Lakes and has beautiful palaces like City Palace and Lake Pichola.

Darjeeling is famous for tea gardens, toy train, and views of Kanchenjunga.

Ooty is a hill station in Tamil Nadu known for botanical gardens, lakes, and cool climate.

Andaman Islands are known for beaches, scuba diving, and crystal-clear water.

Coorg is known for coffee plantations, waterfalls, and scenic landscapes.

Amritsar is famous for the Golden Temple and Punjabi culture.

Hyderabad is known for Charminar, biryani, and IT industry.

Bangalore is known as the Silicon Valley of India and has a pleasant climate.

Kolkata is known for culture, literature, and Howrah Bridge.
